# Trademarks

## The names and logos

- **BotOpsy** is an independent software brand: the publishing name under which hclaro's author releases software, used as an umbrella for several products. It is not a company, corporation or other separate legal entity. Its lowercase form `botopsy` is the GitHub organization name.
- **hclaro** is a BotOpsy product: a local-first workspace for Terraform and OpenTofu. It is written in lowercase, as in its logo, and capitalized as "Hclaro" only where a sentence or title requires it.
- The **hclaro logos** are the `> hclaro` wordmark with the hollow cursor and the `h` app mark.

These names and logos identify BotOpsy and the official hclaro project and its releases. None of them is a registered trademark.

## What is fine without asking

- Saying that your software is based on, derived from, compatible with or works with hclaro.
- Referring to hclaro and BotOpsy by name in articles, talks, documentation, reviews and comparisons.
- Redistributing **unmodified** official hclaro releases under their own name.

## Please do not

- Distribute a modified version under the name "hclaro", or use the hclaro logos for it, in a way that suggests it is the official project. Give your fork its own name and logo.
- Suggest that BotOpsy or the hclaro project endorses or sponsors you or your product.
- Use "hclaro" or "BotOpsy", or the logos, as the name of your own hosted service, company or product.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask.
